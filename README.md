# low-resource-word-matcher
A spelling similarity matcher/detector geared toward finding cognates, spelling variants, and misspellings in low-resource and endangered languages.

For the Cherokee portion of this project, all words from my flashcard deck are found either in the open online Cherokee language lecture courses offered by the Cherokee Nation of Oklahoma, or the Durbin Feeling dictionary at cherokeedictionary.net, or both. I am not indigenous nor do I claim to be an authority on the language: I am only am outside learner.
